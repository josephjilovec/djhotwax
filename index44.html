One name. Four worlds.

Genre is the palette.
Atmosphere is the signature.

DJ Hotwax is not locked to one sound. The through-line is mood: polished, cinematic music with heat in the grooves and a sense of place. Every release belongs to one of four distinct worlds.

Velvet Rooms

French jazz · Cuban nights · hot jazz

Hip-hop · trap-soul · instrumental beats

04

Night Current

House · trance · electronic fusion

Long-form energy for the part of the night that does not want to end.
